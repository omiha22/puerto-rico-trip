KEEP

Historic streets, San Cristóbal, El Morro, San Juan Gate, city walls, Paseo de la Princesa and waterfront. Add the 12-minute public ferry across San Juan Bay to Cataño for waterfront views/local atmosphere.

TIP-BASED TOUR OPTION: Old San Juan Free Walking Tour generally runs at 10 AM for about 2 hours from Plaza Colón. Advance booking required; operator suggests about $10/person. Use this only if we want guided history—the same area can be explored DIY for free.

⏱ ½–full day · 🚕 Uber ~20–30 min or 🚌 bus ~45–60 min · 🎟 forts $10/adult; Ariel free; bring America the Beautiful pass · ⛴ ferry $0.50 adult each way

Family coastal biking

VERIFIED / KEEP · CALLE LOÍZA PICKUP

Cultura Bicicleta at Taller Comunidad La Goyco, 1763 Calle Loíza, has city bikes plus kids bikes. Current rates: $5/hour, $35 for 8 hours, $39 for 24 hours. For 3 bikes, four hours bought hourly is only $60 total; an 8-hour day is $105 total.

Best 4-hour plan: Uber from Coral Beach to Calle Loíza pickup → ride west through the Condado/Atlantic coastal corridor toward Escambrón → use the Paseo Puerta de Tierra, which was designed with an independent bike lane connecting Condado toward Old San Juan → scenic stop around Capitol/Puerta de Tierra → turn around and return bikes. Add inexpensive lunch/coffee on Calle Loíza before or after.

Safety accepted for this planner. Use the coastal/protected routing and avoid improvising onto faster traffic streets. Confirm Ariel's physical bike fit at pickup. This is much better value than $10/hour at Piñones.

Food layer · matched to where we already plan to be

This is the first food layer, not the final restaurant list. Goal: local/value food for adults + at least one simple Ariel option, without crossing town just to eat. We will add exact prices and more market/kiosk choices next.

🏠 Coral Beach / Isla Verde

GROCERY: SuperMax – Isla Verde and Pueblo are full supermarkets; use these to stock condo breakfast, fruit, water, beach lunches and Ariel basics instead of buying every meal out.

Panadería España — strongest practical all-purpose stop: coffee, breakfast, fresh bread/pastries, sandwiches, soups/hot plates and takeout. Current published hours are generally 6 AM–10 PM; good for beach food or grabbing food before an outing.

Piu Bello — easy family backup for breakfast/coffee/gelato, plus burgers/pizza/simple food that works for Ariel.

Mi Casita — nearby Puerto Rican comfort food/mofongo; convenient but recent reviews are inconsistent, so keep as backup rather than a destination meal.

🏰 Old San Juan / Cataño day

GROCERY: U Go Market or Papo's Grocery for cold drinks, fruit, snacks and picnic supplies while walking Old San Juan.

LOCAL VALUE: Deaverdura is the stronger authentic-food choice: small, casual Puerto Rican cooking, roughly $10–20, and repeatedly recommended by locals/recent travelers for good-value comida criolla. DesHistoria: Birra & Empanadas is the cheap hole-in-the-wall-style stop: roughly $4 empanadas and local beer, useful for a snack/light meal.

Café Manolín stays as a practical breakfast/lunch backup, but recent local feedback is mixed on whether it still feels genuinely local versus tourist-heavy.

🎨 Santurce / Calle Loíza

GROCERY/MARKET: La Placita's daytime produce/meat vendors can supply fruit/snacks; for a full condo grocery run, buy near Coral Beach rather than carrying groceries across town.

HOLE-IN-THE-WALL / LOCAL: La Alcapurria Quemá by La Placita is a small casual Puerto Rican fritura spot ($10–20), especially good for fresh alcapurrias. Café Mariam is an inexpensive everyday cafeteria ($1–10) for Puerto Rican breakfast/lunch. La Casita Blanca is the famous old-school neighborhood fonda if we want a fuller traditional meal.

La Preñá – Calle Loíza — inexpensive stuffed arepas; good casual family stop while walking the neighborhood.

Kasalta — bakery/café option closer to Ocean Park with coffee, pastries, sandwiches and plenty of simple choices. Useful if our route drifts east toward the beach rather than La Placita.

🌳 El Yunque · PR-191 corridor

COREUNIQUE TO PR

Puerto Rico's tropical rainforest: mountain viewpoints, waterfalls and short trails. Go early because parking capacity on the main recreation corridor is limited.

🚗 ~45–60 min from Coral Beach · ⏱ 2–4 hr depending hikes · 🎟 main forest sightseeing is DIY/low-cost · 🚘 PR-66 faster/tolled; PR-3 toll-free

CORESWIMMING

Freshwater pools formed by the Fajardo River, with waterfalls and natural rock slides near Las Tinajas. Several Charco Frío pools are shallow enough for families; avoid jumping/rope-swing behavior with Ariel.

📍 Carr. 971, Ceiba · 🚗 ~50–60 min from Coral Beach · ⏱ ~2–3 hr · 🎟 no verified government admission charge; access/parking charge can vary · ⚠️ skip after heavy rain / unsafe river flow

🗿 Caguana Indigenous Ceremonial Park

CORETAÍNO

Major Taíno ceremonial complex with bateyes/plazas, carved stones and petroglyphs. This gives Ariel context for Puerto Rico before Spanish colonization without building the day around a museum.

📍 Utuado · 🚗 ~1h35–1h50 from Coral Beach · 🕐 current tourism listing: daily 8:30 AM–4 PM · 🎟 low-cost; current published prices vary by source, so verify at entry

🌿 Hacienda Buena Vista

STRONG IF TOUR AVAILABLE

Historic estate above Ponce where the story is water power, agriculture and the people who worked the hacienda. The traditional interpretation includes historic buildings, canal system and unusual machinery; Para la Naturaleza also runs cacao-focused programs here.

📍 PR-123 Km 16.8, Ponce · 🎟 official reservation system lists $15 adult / $11 student or 65+ = ~$41 family for many programs · reservation/calendar varies · call/reserve through Para la Naturaleza when a suitable tour appears
